**Oak** | Seed | $60M
[MANDATE FIT: Cyber]
- What they do: AI-native identity security platform that replaces fragmented legacy identity governance tools with a single control plane governing every identity (human, machine, and AI agent) across an organization.
- Investors: Co-led by Accel, CRV, and Greylock Partners. Participation from AlphaDrive Ventures, Hetz Ventures, and angel investors.

## SECTION 3: MARKET INSIGHTS

- **Cybersecurity and identity dominate:** Three of the six deals this week (Oak, Beacon Security, Horizon Trade's security-background team) touch cybersecurity or identity. The category pulled $2.57B in H1 2026 alone (34% of all Israeli tech capital). The emerging sub-theme is identity infrastructure for AI agents, with both Oak ($60M) and NewCore ($66M in June) attacking this space.

- **HealthTech signal strengthening:** Hemispheric ($52M) and TytoCare ($25M+) represent two different vectors: frontier AI models applied to clinical neuroscience, and mature remote diagnostics companies pivoting to AI-first workflows. Both sit squarely in Menomadin's mandate.

- **Defense-tech fundraising accelerating:** Skapion ($36M seed) follows Traysar ($25M in late June) in the defense-tech category. Israeli defense startups collectively raised $846M in H1 2026, nearly matching all of 2025. Investor appetite from US-based VCs (Khosla, UP.Partners, Lux Capital) entering Israeli defense is a structural shift.

- **Seed rounds are getting larger:** Oak at $60M, Hemispheric at $52M, and Skapion at $36M are all technically seed or early-stage rounds. The floor for a meaningful Israeli cybersecurity seed has shifted decisively above $10M. This compresses Series A timing and reshapes co-investment dynamics.

- **Repeat founders continue to command premium terms:** Oak (Shai Morag, Ermetic exit to Tenable for $265M), Beacon Security (Gal Tal-Hochberg, HiredScore exit to Workday for $520M), and Hemispheric (Gidi Littwin, RealFace exit to Apple) all feature founders with prior successful exits. The pattern is consistent: repeat founders raise larger rounds at higher valuations with top-tier investors.

- Beacon Security occupies the "data layer for agentic cyber" niche, which is complementary to both Oak and NewCore rather than competitive. Could be an infrastructure bet that wins regardless of which identity platform prevails.

**High-Priority Opportunities for Outreach This Week:**
- **Beacon Security** is the strongest fit for Menomadin's mandate: cybersecurity, seed stage, repeat founder with a major exit, 300% ARR growth, and at $13M raised the company likely has room for Series A co-investment at Menomadin's check size. Recommend immediate outreach to Gal Tal-Hochberg.
- **Hemispheric** represents a frontier healthtech opportunity where Menomadin's impact thesis (brain health, neuroscience) could differentiate it from purely financial investors. The $52M already raised may limit Series A co-investment, but worth exploring the cap table structure.

**Hailo Acquisition Watch:**
- Israeli edge AI chipmaker Hailo, once valued at $1B+, is in advanced talks to sell to an undisclosed US chipmaker for tens of millions. This represents a cautionary signal about hardware-first AI plays where market timing proved premature. The lesson: the edge AI inference market is real but adoption timelines were overestimated by 3-5 years.
