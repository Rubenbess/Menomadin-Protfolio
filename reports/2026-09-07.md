# Menomadin Weekly Deal Report

**Week of August 31 - September 7, 2026**

Israeli and Israeli-founded startups announced approximately $1.4 billion across 14 funding rounds this week, more than twice the $577.5 million raised during the entire month of August. Four rounds above $150 million accounted for 84% of the total. The concentration of capital in AI, cybersecurity and defense reflects a broader market pattern: more money entering Israeli tech, but flowing to fewer, larger companies.

---

## Section 1: Top Deals

**Wonderful** | Series C | $550M
[MANDATE FIT: AI]
- What they do: Enterprise AI operating system that unifies fragmented AI agent deployments into a single orchestration layer. Co-founded in early 2025 by Bar Winkler and Roey Lalazar, headquartered in the Netherlands with Israeli founders.
- Investors: Insight Partners (lead), Salesforce, Index Ventures, IVP, Vine Ventures, 9Yards, Bessemer Venture Partners
- Why it matters: Doubling valuation from $2B to $5B in under six months signals that enterprise AI infrastructure is entering a winner-take-most phase. Wonderful reached ~$70M ARR and is tracking to $100M by year-end, just 20 months after founding.
- Signals: Salesforce participating alongside Insight suggests deep strategic alignment. The speed of valuation growth is unusual even for AI.
- Suggested angle: Too large for direct investment, but Wonderful's expansion creates demand for complementary tooling in AI security, compliance and vertical AI agents. Track the ecosystem forming around it.

**Upwind** | Series C | $300M
[MANDATE FIT: Cyber]
- What they do: AI-powered cloud security platform providing runtime protection for cloud applications. Founded in 2022 by Amiram Shachar, Tal Zur, Lavi Ferdman and Liran Polak, the founding team behind Spot.io (acquired by NetApp in 2020).
- Investors: Bessemer Venture Partners and TCV (co-leads), Salesforce Ventures, Greylock, Craft Ventures, Cyberstarts, Leaders Fund, Alta Park Capital, Penny Jar Capital
- Why it matters: Valuation jumped from ~$1.8B to $3.8B in five months. Cloud security continues to consolidate around companies that can provide runtime context for AI workloads. Repeat founder team from Spot.io adds execution credibility.
- Signals: TCV participation (growth-stage specialist) alongside Bessemer indicates a company with strong unit economics. Salesforce Ventures appearing in both this round and HiBob suggests a coordinated Israeli expansion strategy.
- Suggested angle: Track adjacent cloud security startups at earlier stages that Upwind might acquire or that address gaps in its platform.

**HiBob** | Growth | $166M
[MANDATE FIT: B2B SaaS]
- What they do: Cloud-based HR and workforce management platform serving mid-market companies. Israeli HQ, Tel Aviv. Plans to use new capital for acquisitions and AI-powered workplace tools.
- Investors: Salesforce (lead, majority of capital), Farallon Capital Management
- Why it matters: Salesforce leading the round is a strategic signal, not just a financial one. HiBob is positioning itself as infrastructure for AI-powered HR, which could redefine workforce management. ARR exceeds $400M, valuation now $3.2B, and the company is exploring acquisitions.
- Signals: Salesforce leading a non-CRM HR round. Potential IPO candidate. Acquisition strategy creates opportunities for early-stage HR-adjacent startups.
- Suggested angle: Monitor HiBob's acquisition targets. Israeli HR-tech and workforce-AI startups at Series A could become targets or ecosystem partners.

**Lyte** | Series C | $165M
[MANDATE FIT: Deeptech, AI]
- What they do: Physical AI perception technology for robotics. Builds sensor systems that enable robots to understand and navigate physical environments. Founded by former Apple/PrimeSense engineers including CEO Alexander Shpunt (PrimeSense founder, acquired by Apple). Chairman is Avigdor Willenz.
- Investors: Maverick Silicon (lead), Fidelity Management & Research, Atreides Management, Key1 Capital, Ora Global
- Why it matters: Tripled valuation to $1.6B. Physical AI perception is becoming the bottleneck for robotics deployment. Lyte's team built the depth-sensing technology behind Apple Face ID, giving them a deep technical moat.
- Signals: Avigdor Willenz as founding investor and chairman is a strong signal. He co-founded Galileo Technology (sold to Marvell for $2.7B) and Annapurna Labs (sold to Amazon). Fidelity staying in from Series B shows institutional conviction.
- Suggested angle: Physical AI is generating a supply chain of specialized component and software companies. Map emerging Israeli robotics-infrastructure startups for Series A opportunities.

**AIR Security** | Seed (two rounds) | $50M
[MANDATE FIT: AI, Cyber]
- What they do: Inline firewall for AI agents. Discovers agents running inside enterprises, continuously vets skills, tools and components those agents use, and blocks interactions with unapproved software or external sources. Founded Feb 2026 by Yair Saban (CEO) and Niv Hoffman (CTO), Unit 8200 veterans.
- Investors: Sequoia Capital ($10M first seed lead), Greenoaks Capital Partners ($40M second seed lead), Swish Ventures, Netz, angel investors
- Why it matters: $50M in seed funding within six months of founding is exceptional. The AI agent security category barely existed a year ago but is now attracting top-tier capital. AIR found 17,800+ public AI add-ons linked to untrusted sources across 6.7M installations.
- Signals: Sequoia leading a first seed for an Israeli cybersecurity startup at this scale. The AI agent supply chain is the new attack surface, similar to how open-source dependencies created the software supply chain security category.
- Suggested angle: Strong mandate fit for Catalyst. While this specific company has raised beyond Series A range, the AI agent security category is nascent. Look for adjacent companies tackling agent governance, compliance and identity at earlier stages.

**AITAN** | Series A | $41M
[MANDATE FIT: Deeptech]
- What they do: Autonomous defense systems for coordinating drones and unmanned vehicles on the battlefield. AI-based command and control platform enabling military forces to operate coordinated drone swarms. Won the IDF's next-generation attack drone C2 tender.
- Investors: Deep33 and Dell Technologies Capital (co-leads), Gokul Rajaram (Pinterest/Coinbase board member), Benjamin Ling (Bling Capital), Kevin Weil (Scribble Ventures)
- Why it matters: AITAN (formerly eyesAtop) was acquired by serial entrepreneurs Udi Oster and Daniel Almog in 2025 and quickly repositioned for the autonomous defense wave. The IDF contract validates the technology. Total raised is $54M.
- Signals: Dell Technologies Capital investing in defense tech. Deep33, a dedicated Israeli deep-tech fund, co-leading. IDF contract as anchor customer.
- Suggested angle: Defense-tech remains a hot Israeli vertical with strong US/NATO demand. Track the autonomous systems supply chain.

**Guardio** | Growth | $40M
[MANDATE FIT: Cyber]
- What they do: Consumer cybersecurity platform protecting email, social media, browsing and devices against scams, phishing, malicious websites and identity theft. Israeli HQ, founded 2018.
- Investors: ION Crossover Partners, Union Tech Ventures, Vintage Investment Partners, Cerca Partners, Emerge Ventures (all existing). Wiz CEO Assaf Rappaport joined as investor.
- Why it matters: Guardio is now valued at $1.1B with $150M ARR and 1M+ customers. Consumer cybersecurity has been a historically difficult category, making Guardio's traction notable. Rappaport's personal investment is a validation signal from the most successful Israeli cyber founder of the current era.
- Signals: Rappaport investing personally. $150M ARR on a consumer cybersecurity model. Total funding $167M.
- Suggested angle: Late-stage, but its growth validates the consumer security thesis. Track B2B/B2C hybrid models in adjacent trust-and-safety categories.

**Huskeys** | Series A | $27M
[MANDATE FIT: Cyber]
- What they do: Edge security platform that adds an agentic AI layer to existing web application firewalls, adapting protection for an internet increasingly shaped by autonomous AI agents. Founded 2025 by IDF Unit 8200 alumni Itai Gafni (CEO) and Roy Weisfeld (CTO).
- Investors: Blackstone Innovations Investments (lead), Skinos Ventures, Shlomo Kramer fund, Okta Ventures, Zscaler, Merlin Ventures, plus angels from Google, Microsoft, Palo Alto Networks, Cloudflare, Check Point, AWS, Intel
- Why it matters: Blackstone leading a Series A in Israeli cyber is noteworthy. The investor roster reads like a who's-who of enterprise security. Customers include TikTok, Legoland, Blackstone itself and Hugging Face. Valuation past $100M.
- Signals: Blackstone as lead. Shlomo Kramer (Check Point, Imperva, Cato Networks co-founder) investing. Okta and Zscaler funds participating signals ecosystem validation.
- Suggested angle: Strong mandate fit for Catalyst at the right stage. The AI-native WAF category is emerging. Huskeys' investor base suggests strong distribution potential.

**Newlight** | Seed | $9M
[MANDATE FIT: CleanTech/Energy]
- What they do: Maritime decarbonisation through hydrogen-hybrid energy systems that retrofit onto existing commercial vessels. Founded by Haran Cohen Hillel and Evyatar Cohen, former senior Israeli Navy officers. Proven 24% fuel reduction and 28% CO2 reduction on an 8,500-nautical-mile commercial voyage.
- Investors: lomarlabs, BIRD Energy (US-Israel joint initiative), Undeterred Capital, CiRi Ventures, Fusion VC
- Why it matters: Shipping accounts for ~3% of global emissions. Newlight's retrofit approach avoids the fleet-replacement problem that plagues most maritime decarbonisation efforts. Real-world validation on a 57,000-DWT bulk carrier is a strong proof point.
- Signals: BIRD Energy (US DOE and Israel Ministry of Energy joint program) backing. Lomar Shipping (fleet operator) investing through lomarlabs, indicating customer-investor alignment.
- Suggested angle: Direct mandate fit for Impact (CleanTech/Energy). Seed stage, $9M raised, Israeli Navy founder team, proven technology. The maritime decarbonisation market is large and underpenetrated. Worth an immediate outreach.

## Section 3: Market Insights

- **AI security is now a standalone category.** Three of the 14 deals this week (AIR Security, Huskeys, Guardio) directly address security challenges created by AI agents and AI-driven threats. Combined, they raised $117M. This is no longer a feature inside existing platforms; it is a category with dedicated capital.

- **Cybersecurity dominance continues.** Seven of 14 deals (Upwind, AIR, Guardio, Huskeys, AITAN, Easy Aerial, Flamingo) touch security or defense, accounting for roughly $478M or 34% of total capital. Israel's position as the global cybersecurity hub is deepening, not plateauing.

- **Repeat founders driving the largest rounds.** The top four rounds by size were all led by repeat founders: Wonderful (Winkler/Lalazar), Upwind (ex-Spot.io team), HiBob (Ronni Zehavi, decade-long operator), Lyte (Alexander Shpunt, PrimeSense/Apple). Investors are concentrating capital in teams with prior exits.

- **Salesforce appeared in three deals.** Salesforce or Salesforce Ventures participated in Wonderful ($550M), Upwind ($300M) and HiBob ($166M), totaling over $1B in rounds. This looks like a coordinated Israeli ecosystem play, likely tied to its AI agent and enterprise platform strategy.

- **Defense tech is attracting new investor profiles.** Dell Technologies Capital co-leading AITAN, Blackstone leading Huskeys, and Insight Partners in Easy Aerial show that defense and security deals are pulling in non-traditional defense investors. The category is mainstreaming.

- **Seed rounds are getting larger.** AIR Security raised $50M across two seeds in six months. DataAgent raised $10M pre-seed. The bar for what constitutes seed-stage capital in Israeli cyber and AI continues to rise, compressing the window for Series A funds to enter.

## Section 4: Special Flags

**Repeat Founders**
- Amiram Shachar (Upwind): Co-founded Spot.io, acquired by NetApp in 2020. Now building Upwind at $3.8B valuation.
- Alexander Shpunt (Lyte): Founded PrimeSense, acquired by Apple. Led development of Face ID depth-sensing technology. Now building physical AI perception at $1.6B.
- Avigdor Willenz (Lyte chairman): Co-founded Galileo Technology (sold to Marvell for $2.7B) and Annapurna Labs (sold to Amazon). Founding investor in Lyte.
- Udi Oster and Daniel Almog (AITAN): Serial defense-tech entrepreneurs who acquired eyesAtop and repositioned it.
- Ishay Yaari and Nati Shalom (DataAgent): Previously built Cloudify, acquired by Dell in 2023.
- Michael Assraf (Flamingo): Co-founded Vicarius in Israel, raised $60M. Now building Flamingo.
- Nir Sabato (Catch): Previously led Weavy, sold to Figma in a $200M exit.

**Competitive Threats to Mandate Sectors**
- AIR Security's $50M seed and Huskeys' $27M Series A in AI agent security could preempt opportunities for other startups in this emerging category. The window for new entrants at fundable stages is narrowing.
- Wonderful's $550M raise and enterprise AI OS positioning could commoditize point solutions in vertical AI, potentially affecting portfolio companies building narrow AI tools.

**High-Priority Opportunities for Outreach This Week**
- **Newlight** (Seed, $9M): Direct Impact mandate fit (CleanTech/Energy). Israeli Navy founders, proven technology with commercial validation, BIRD Energy backing. Maritime decarbonisation is a large, underserved market. Check size ($0.5M-$5M) aligns perfectly with their stage.
- **Huskeys** (Series A, $27M): Catalyst mandate fit (Cyber). While the round is closed, the company is at the right stage for relationship-building ahead of future rounds. Blackstone-led with an exceptional angel roster.
- **Catch** (Seed, $5M): Catalyst mandate fit (AI, B2B SaaS). Early stage, Tel Aviv-based, repeat founder (Weavy/Figma exit). AI executive productivity is a large addressable market. At seed, a Series A will follow.
- **DataAgent** (Pre-Seed, $10M): Catalyst mandate fit (AI, B2B SaaS). Tel Aviv-based, repeat founders (ex-Cloudify/Dell). Self-healing infrastructure is a growing category. Pre-seed means Series A is 12-18 months out. Worth tracking.
