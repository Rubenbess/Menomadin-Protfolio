# Menomadin Investments Weekly Deal Report

**Week of September 14 -- September 21, 2026**

Israeli startups announced $277 million across five confirmed funding rounds this week, alongside a $300M+ exit. The week's activity was led by Buildots, which approached unicorn status on the back of the data center construction boom. AI agent security, industrial AI, and education technology rounded out the deal flow. The exit market stayed active with OpenAI's acquisition of Israeli-founded Glass Imaging.

---

## Section 1: Top Deals

**Buildots** | Growth Capital | $130M
- What they do: AI-powered construction management platform that uses computer vision to convert site video into continuously updated digital models, comparing imagery to schedules and 3D building plans to classify completed work, spot deviations, and forecast delays.
- Investors: OG Venture Partners (lead, Eyal Ofer), Lightspeed Venture Partners, Intel Capital, Mohari Ventures, Human Capital, Qumra Capital, Viola Growth, Poalim Equity, Avigdor Willenz
- Why it matters: The data center construction boom is creating massive demand for tools that can reduce delays and cost overruns on billion-dollar builds. Buildots is now the go-to platform for hyperscalers, chip fabs, and hospitals.
- Signals: Total funding reaches $297M since 2018 founding. Company approaching $1B valuation. CEO Roy Danon has signaled unicorn milestone is imminent.
- Suggested angle: Late stage for direct investment, but signals growing appetite for ConTech/AI. Track earlier-stage companies building adjacent tools for the physical-economy AI stack.

**MIND** | Series B | $72M
[MANDATE FIT: Cyber]
- What they do: AI-native data loss prevention platform that protects sensitive enterprise data as employees and AI agents move it across systems, GenAI services, and third-party tools.
- Investors: Crosspoint Capital Partners (lead), YL Ventures, Paladin Capital Group
- Why it matters: As enterprises adopt agentic AI, the attack surface for data exfiltration expands dramatically. MIND reported 17x revenue growth and 8x customer growth in the past year, validating the category.
- Signals: Valuation tripled to $300M. Founded 2023, R&D center in Israel. The DLP-for-AI-agents thesis is attracting tier-1 cyber investors.
- Suggested angle: Series B puts MIND past Menomadin's sweet spot, but the category is ripe. Scan for pre-seed and seed competitors building data governance for agentic environments.

**Noetive** | Seed | $41M
[MANDATE FIT: AI / Deeptech]
- What they do: Building AI agents and proprietary sensing hardware for industrial operations. The platform creates "world models" for factories, logistics networks, energy systems, and data centers, functioning as an intelligence-of-record for the physical economy.
- Investors: Eclipse (lead), Craft Ventures, The Westly Group, Swish Ventures, Factory, Incite Ventures, Gigascale Capital, Operator Partners, Liquid 2 Ventures
- Why it matters: $41M is an unusually large seed, signaling strong conviction. The "Full Self-Driving for industrial ops" framing positions Noetive at the intersection of physical AI and enterprise infrastructure.
- Signals: Founders Amir Frenkel (ex-Meta VP, nearly a decade) and Dan Barak (ex-Lyft, ex-Stackbit/Netlify) met during Israeli military service. Based in SF with Israeli roots.
- Suggested angle: Too early for a Series A check today, but the Catalyst mandate should track Noetive's progress. Industrial AI is an emerging cluster worth mapping.

**Loora** | Series B | $22M
[MANDATE FIT: Education]
- What they do: AI-powered English tutoring app that provides personalized spoken-English practice, adapting conversations to proficiency level and delivering real-time feedback on grammar, pronunciation, and fluency. 15 million users worldwide.
- Investors: Union Tech Ventures (co-lead), Vgames (co-lead), Emerge, Hearst Ventures, QP Ventures
- Why it matters: English proficiency remains one of the largest barriers to economic mobility globally. Loora's 15M-user base demonstrates product-market fit in a category with massive TAM and social impact.
- Signals: Total funding reaches $43M. Israeli-founded. Expanding across US, Europe, Asia, and South America.
- Suggested angle: Series B is past the Impact mandate's primary entry point, but Loora validates the AI-for-education thesis. High relevance for Menomadin's Education vertical. Explore whether earlier-stage competitors exist in adjacent language or skills-training categories.

**Keewano** | Seed | $12M
[MANDATE FIT: AI]
- What they do: Built KeewanoDB, an event-oriented database designed for AI agent reasoning. Conventional databases handle structured queries from people; Keewano's architecture lets AI agents examine behavioral sequences and determine causation in real time.
- Investors: Hetz Ventures (lead), a16z speedrun, Remagine Ventures, DIG Ventures
- Why it matters: AI agent infrastructure is an emerging category, and purpose-built databases are a critical missing layer. Keewano addresses a genuine architectural gap.
- Signals: Tel Aviv-based. Repeat founders: team previously built TestProject (acquired by Tricentis, 2019) and Devalore (acquired by Abra, 2022). a16z's speedrun program participation signals strong US investor interest.
- Suggested angle: Seed stage with repeat founders and a16z backing. Could be a Catalyst-track prospect if the next round is a $5M+ Series A. Worth a direct conversation.

**Glass Imaging (Exit)** | Acquisition by OpenAI | $300M+
- What they do: Developed AI-powered image-processing software for smartphone cameras, using computational imaging and edge AI to correct optical imperfections and recover fine textures in real time.
- Israeli connection: Founded by Ziv Attar, a former Apple engineer who led the team behind iPhone Portrait Mode, and Tom Bishop (also ex-Apple). Israeli-founded.
- Why it matters: 3x markup from the 2025 funding round ($100M valuation). Signals OpenAI's hardware ambitions and willingness to pay premium for Israeli deep-tech teams.

## Section 3: Market Insights

- **AI agent infrastructure is the category of the moment.** Three of five deals this week (MIND, Keewano, Noetive) directly serve the agentic AI stack: data protection, purpose-built databases, and physical-world reasoning. This mirrors the broader September trend where AIR Security ($50M), Cymphony ($25M), Euno ($23M), and Zenity ($125M) all raised in adjacent spaces.

- **Construction and physical-economy AI is heating up.** Buildots' $130M round and Noetive's $41M seed together represent $171M flowing into AI for the built environment. The data center construction boom is pulling capital into a sector that was previously underfunded relative to its TAM.

- **Education technology is back on the radar.** Loora's $22M Series B and 15M-user milestone suggest renewed investor appetite for EdTech with defensible AI moats. The company's social-impact angle (economic mobility through English proficiency) aligns directly with Menomadin's Impact mandate.

- **Israeli founders continue to build outside Israel.** Noetive (SF), MIND (Seattle), and Keewano (Tel Aviv with US-facing GTM) reflect a persistent pattern: Israeli-origin founding teams increasingly headquarter or co-locate in the US while maintaining Israeli R&D.

- **Exit market remains active.** OpenAI's $300M+ acquisition of Glass Imaging at a 3x premium to the prior round signals strong demand for Israeli deep-tech talent, particularly in areas adjacent to AI model development.

## Section 4: Special Flags

- **Repeat founders at Keewano.** The founding team previously built and exited TestProject (acquired by Tricentis, 2019) and Devalore (acquired by Abra, 2022). Two successful exits in five years is a strong signal. Combined with Hetz and a16z backing, this is worth tracking for the Catalyst mandate.

- **Competitive signal in AI agent security.** MIND's $72M round adds to a wave of Israeli AI-agent-security companies raising significant capital (Zenity $125M in August, Cymphony $25M, AIR Security $50M, Onyx $113M in July). The category is forming fast, valuations are rising, and the window for early-stage entry is narrowing. Menomadin's Cyber track should map the remaining white space.

- **High-priority opportunity: Loora.** While the Series B is past the typical Impact entry, Loora's Education mandate fit, Israeli HQ, and social-impact thesis make it worth a conversation. If the company raises a growth round, co-investment or secondary participation could be viable.

- **High-priority opportunity: Keewano.** $12M seed, Tel Aviv-based, repeat founders, a16z backing, building AI infrastructure. If the Series A materializes in the $5M+ range, this fits the Catalyst mandate directly. Recommend outreach this week.
