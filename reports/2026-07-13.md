## Section 1: Top Deals

**Skapion** | Seed | $36M
- What they do: Developing the first native counter-swarm defense system designed to detect, engage, and neutralize drone and UAV attacks at scale, significantly reducing interception costs compared with conventional air defense.
- Investors: UP.Partners and Khosla Ventures (co-leads), Fusion VC, Stratos Ventures, TBD VC, q Fund
- Why it matters: The largest seed round of the week signals growing investor conviction in counter-drone technology as mass UAV attacks become a defining feature of modern warfare. Khosla's entry into Israeli defense tech is notable.
- Signals: Founding team includes Brig. Gen. (res.) Pini Yungman, former head of air and missile defense at Rafael, who led development of Iron Dome and David's Sling. HQ in Washington D.C. with R&D in Ramat Gan positions the company for U.S. defense procurement.
- Suggested angle: Defense tech falls outside Menomadin's core mandate, but the adjacency to critical infrastructure protection and the caliber of the founding team make this worth tracking as a market signal for Israeli defense tech momentum.

**Velocity** | Seed | $27M
[MANDATE FIT: AI]
- What they do: Building monetization infrastructure for AI-native software applications, addressing rising compute costs and the challenge of converting free AI users into paying customers through intent-driven advertising and revenue generation tools.
- Investors: NFX and Red Dot Capital Partners (co-leads), Stardom Ventures, Corner Ventures, Transcend. Angel investors include founders of EON, SuperPlay, Tripledot Studios, and the co-founders of ironSource/Zyg.
- Why it matters: The AI application layer is maturing past pure model development into revenue infrastructure. Velocity's thesis that AI companies face a monetization gap is well-timed as hundreds of AI startups search for sustainable business models beyond subscription pricing.
- Signals: Repeat founders from ironSource and Unity with deep expertise in developer monetization and growth infrastructure. CEO Tal Shoham, COO Amir Shaked, and CPO Nimrod Zota spent over a decade together building the advertising and growth stack that ironSource scaled before its $4.4B merger with Unity.
- Suggested angle: Strong mandate fit in AI infrastructure. The repeat-founder profile and oversubscribed round suggest high conviction. Worth engaging early as the company is pre-product-market-fit and could benefit from strategic value-add investors.

**Alta** | Series A | $25M
[MANDATE FIT: AI, B2B SaaS]
- What they do: AI-native operating layer for revenue teams, replacing fragmented marketing and sales toolchains with autonomous agents that handle prospecting, qualification, outreach, and pipeline management for B2B go-to-market operations.
- Investors: IN Venture / Sumitomo Corporation (lead), Mindset Ventures, Skywell Capital, Leumitech77, Entree Capital, Target Global, Verissimo Ventures
- Why it matters: 800% revenue growth in under a year and a $15M revenue run rate at Series A are exceptional metrics. The entry of Sumitomo's investment arm signals growing Japanese institutional interest in Israeli B2B SaaS.
- Signals: Founded by Stav Levi-Neumark and Tom Hoffen, both former monday.com employees, alongside serial entrepreneur Mor Shabtai. Total raised now $32M. The company targets $30M revenue by end of 2026 with only 80 employees, pointing to capital-efficient growth.
- Suggested angle: Direct mandate fit across AI and B2B SaaS. Series A stage with strong revenue traction is within Menomadin's investment window. The team's monday.com pedigree and rapid scaling warrant a closer look. Consider outreach this week.

---

**Tangos AI** | Seed | $20M
[MANDATE FIT: Fintech, AI]
- What they do: Autonomous AI platform for financial crime investigations. Unlike traditional compliance tools that flag potential risks, Tangos performs full investigative workflows: evaluating evidence, testing hypotheses, validating findings, and producing case files for human review.
- Investors: Red Dot Capital Partners (lead), Leaders Fund, Clarim, Venture Israel, Signal Fire, Clutch Capital, Selah Ventures, Bright Data (strategic)
- Why it matters: $100M valuation at seed reflects the premium the market places on experienced founders tackling regulated, high-stakes verticals with AI. Financial crime generates $1.5T in illicit proceeds annually, and compliance teams face rising alert volumes with a global shortage of experienced investigators.
- Signals: CEO Eyal Azoulay is a serial entrepreneur with three prior exits, including one acquired by BNY. The combination of deep domain expertise and AI-native architecture positions Tangos to capture the compliance automation wave.
- Suggested angle: Strong mandate fit across Fintech and AI. The $20M seed and $100M valuation are at the upper end but below Menomadin's typical Series A entry. Worth building a relationship now for potential participation in a future Series A, especially given the regulatory tailwinds driving demand.

## Section 3: Market Insights

- **AI infrastructure dominated the week.** Three of four confirmed deals (Velocity, Alta, Tangos) are building AI-native platforms for specific verticals: monetization, go-to-market, and compliance. This continues the H1 2026 trend where AI companies increasingly compete on domain-specific application layers rather than foundational models.

- **Red Dot Capital Partners appeared in two of four deals** (Velocity and Tangos), reinforcing its position as one of the most active early-stage investors in the Israeli AI ecosystem this month. Their dual bets on AI monetization and AI compliance suggest a thesis around AI operational infrastructure.

- **Defense tech continues its 2026 surge.** Skapion's $36M seed, backed by Khosla Ventures and tied to Iron Dome alumni, follows $846M raised by defense and quantum companies in H1 2026, nearly matching the full-year 2025 total of $953M. The Stratos Ventures $50M fund (announced June 25) is already deploying into this week's deals.

- **Repeat founders command premium valuations.** All four deals feature experienced founding teams: ex-ironSource (Velocity), ex-monday.com (Alta), three-exit serial entrepreneur (Tangos), and former Rafael general (Skapion). The market is clearly rewarding operator pedigree, consistent with the H1 2026 trend of fewer but larger rounds going to proven teams.

- **Government policy tailwind approaching.** Starting July 15, the Israel Innovation Authority will increase maximum investment caps for DeepTech companies at Pre-Seed (NIS 1.5M to 2M) and Seed (NIS 5M to 6M), with maximum eligible round sizes rising to NIS 30M (~$10M). This could expand the pipeline of early-stage Israeli deep tech companies in coming quarters.

- **H1 2026 context.** Israeli startups raised $8.4-8.6B in H1 2026, up 45-52% year-over-year, but with 35% fewer rounds. Israel produced 16 new unicorns in H1, a record pace. Israel ranked #1 globally for AI-native startups at 31%, ahead of the U.S. at 30%.
